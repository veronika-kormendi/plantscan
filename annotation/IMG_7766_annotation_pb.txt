SPARKLING GLUCOSE DRINK WITH SUGARS
AND SWEETENERS
INGREDIENTS: Carbonated Water, Glucose Syrup (13%),
Acids (Citric Acid, Lactic Acid), Flavourings,  Acidity
Regulator (Sodium Citrate), Preservative (Potassium
Sorbate), Caffeine, Sweeteners (Sucralose, Acesulfame
K), Antioxidant (Ascorbic Acid), Colours (Sunset Yellow,
Ponceau 4R), Niacin (Vitamin B3).

effect on activity and attention in children.
